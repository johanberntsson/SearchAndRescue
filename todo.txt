  shorten the step growth.
- no pitch control yet, and the horizon is fixed.
- more speed is available if wanted: the sky fill is ~12000 pixels a frame at
  around 25 cycles each and could be unrolled, and biasing the height

  battery, GPS and signal strength.

DONE:

- install calypsi C (rpm) from https://github.com/hth313/Calypsi-tool-chains/releases, and "make run" to run a simple hello world in src/main.c
- build to a d81 with the game as autoboot.c65 and the maps as separate files
  on the same disk, run with xemu-xmega65 -8
- minimal voxel engine you can fly around in: 160x192 full-colour display
  hardware-stretched to 320 wide, double buffered, 256x256 height and colour
  maps loaded off the disk. ASWD to fly, R/F for altitude.
- profiler (src/profile.c, tools/profread.py) with a clock calibrated against
  the raster, plus an always-on FPS counter in the corner
- performance: 0.74 fps -> 10.3 fps. The 32-bit multiply the compiler emitted
  was 64% of the frame; the hardware multiplier and then an assembly inner
  loop (src/voxel_asm.s) did most of the rest.
